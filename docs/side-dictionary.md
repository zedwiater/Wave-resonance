## FALSE FRIENDS — same word, different mechanism (do not trip these in public)

1. **Precipitation (Voit)** = baryonic gas cooling out of a hot halo at t_cool ≈ 10 t_ff, raining onto a black hole, driving feedback. Prestigious, established — and NOT twist shed from a superfluid filament. Unqualified use will be read as Voit's mechanism.
2. **Scaffolding (NASA)** = ordinary cold dark matter. Borrowing the word borrows CDM's meaning unless the superfluid difference is stated.
3. **Daughters / merger trees** run bottom-up (small merges into large). His daughters shed downward and expand again — a deliberate inversion; unflagged, it reads as an error.
4. **Landau criterion ≠ spin condition.** The published mover condition is velocity. Claiming literature support for a spin requirement would be wrong; the search found none.
5. **Spin–filament alignment ≠ spin condition.** Orientation of galaxy spin vs its filament — different claim entirely.
6. **Supersolid ≠ any frozen web.** A supersolid is a specific phase (density modulation + global phase coherence). A frozen filament network is a solid or pinned vortex array unless the coherence is shown.
7. **Toroidal-poloidal** in plasma physics describes magnetic geometry, not superfluid flow — fine to use, better to know.
