## Part 2 — The logic (framework, with falsifiers)

**The spectrum lands whole.** A daughter tier does not receive a narrowed
staircase of light; it receives the full span, and tiers slide the
distribution's interaction *peak* without narrowing its width. Visible
light is one tier's coupling band, not a survivor. *Falsifier:* a tier
whose measured spectrum narrows as its peak slides.

**The line is a marginality axis.** Read λ = 0 as the critical line:
modes on it neither grow nor decay — they persist. Daughters inherit the
spectrum for free; they do **not** inherit the zeros. Each tier's set of
on-line modes is re-earned by its own transduction. Off-line modes run
away or ring down. *Falsifier (in-sim, being run as iteration 13
Waves C/D):* after the full cascade, the daughter stack's persistent
configurations scatter off marginality instead of concentrating on it.

**Encasement makes spectra.** No boundaries, no standing waves, no
discrete modes. The critical strip is the worked example: for Re(s) > 1
the Euler product rules (a deterministic ledger), for Re(s) ≤ 0 the
trivial zeros rule (another), and ζ's only pole sits on the boundary at
s = 1. The strip is exactly where both ledgers fail — the only room where
a spectrum has to be *earned* dynamically instead of read off.

**Coupling is a validated transaction.** Not a coefficient: a request,
a gate decision (the decision's duration is the dwell), a discrete
carrier exchange, and a receipt. The shape is scale-free and the bottom
and top are known physics: at the elementary scale, a joining is granted
only if a partner can balance energy, momentum, charge, and angular
momentum (pair production in this program requires the dense wave field
as momentum partner, and cross-sections scale with local stacking
density — crowded zones grant requests, thin zones refuse them). At the
cosmic scale the same shape is the Jeans criterion: a gas cloud's
collapse is a request granted only when self-gravity beats pressure
support. Between those floors: electron exchange, stripping, and receipt
are the atomic couple; ion cascades are the physiological one.

**Gravity is parking validation.** A configuration that passes validation
parks — persists at marginality — and then participates: it can draw from
and feed its host eddy. Validation is not free; the fee is booked
(Landauer line) and paid from the configuration's own budget. *Falsifier:*
a validation rule that parks configurations off the marginality axis as
readily as on it (the scrambled control already shows the contrast:
locking without marginality).

**Return has two routes, and the rectifier recirculates.** Bleed-back:
slow, diffuse, earned when a configuration resonates enough power *and*
data. Rectification: directed export through a transducer — and the
rectifier also recirculates, at quasar/starburst scale and at the
single-photon stripping pass alike. There is no contradiction with the
one-way element iteration 12 demands: one-way-ness lives in the
*transformation*, not in a wall. What returns is not what left — a photon
that passes a transducer and escapes with new speed, phase, and twist
carries the receipt that exchange happened. *Falsifier:* the
changed-triple rate across transducer passes must beat the
changed-triple rate across empty propagation of the same length
(dispersion alone changes phase; if the rates match, the receipt is
void). Exchange also couples inductively in a band around the line, not
only exactly on it — the band's width is currently a gate choice
(ε = 0.005), not a measured coupling width, and is flagged as unmeasured
until return events are booked against parent |λ|.

Earlier framing smeared gravity into the encasement. Corrected, in the
author's terms:

  herding. The flow field IS the gravity.

  poloidal and toroidal flow, momentum transport building force and
  acceleration. Named straggler in the current engine: its gravity is a
  bolt-on attraction factor, not emergent from light-in-flow — the
